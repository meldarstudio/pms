Main guest journey. Payment outcomes are reconciled from the checkout; reopening or reloading resumes that attempt.

A new search clears the rate-selection step. Sold-out, occupancy-limited, and offline rooms do not enter rate selection. Date-picker state is independent of offer loading.

PayStep is the operation in progress, separate from the payment outcome. A busy step rejects another Pay request. A changed quote, including a lower price, requires fresh acceptance.

These are reconciliation paths, not a mandatory linear sequence. Polling can observe the final outcome directly. Bank authentication uses the card stored for the matching attempt.
